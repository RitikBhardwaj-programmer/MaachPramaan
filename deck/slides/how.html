How the survey works

1 · FREEZE

Pond list and seed

List hashed; seed committed before selection

2 · SELECT

~200 ponds per district

Groups by type, size, intensity; largest farms all taken

3 · RECORD

Diary per harvest

Phone form, under two minutes; monthly check

4 · WEIGH

Subsample checked

Enumerators weigh harvests on ~30% of farms, more if needed

5 · ESTIMATE

Total ± interval

Diary total × weighed/diary ratio, plus missing ponds

Missing ponds: 1 km² grid cells are fully listed to measure and add ponds the list misses.

Dropouts: farmers who stop the diary still answer a year-end recall, which stands in for them.

Field capture on KoboToolbox / ODK Collect (offline); estimation engine in open-source Python

Five steps. The innovation is not any one step but the chain: a probability sample, per-harvest diaries, and weighing that measures the diary error instead of assuming it. The interval comes from a bootstrap that re-runs the whole pipeline.
