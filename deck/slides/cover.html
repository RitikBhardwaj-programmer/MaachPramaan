Maach Pramaan

Counting Assam’s fish, with a margin of error

A scientific sample survey for pond and tank aquaculture production, open and re-runnable by anyone

Ritik Bhardwaj · B.Tech. IT, NIT Srinagar

All results shown are from simulation. No real Assam data has been used.

Maach Pramaan means “fish proof”: maach is the everyday Assamese word for fish, as in the department’s own “Ghare Ghare Pukhuri, Ghare Ghare Maach”. One sentence pitch: Assam’s pond and tank production is estimated today as area times one productivity figure, with no margin of error. We replace it with a sample survey whose estimate carries a 95% interval and can be re-run by anyone.
